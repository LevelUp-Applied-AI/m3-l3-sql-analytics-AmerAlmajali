# KPI Brief — Levant Tech Solutions

## KPI 1: Department Salary Efficiency

**Definition:** Total salary of a department ÷ Number of employees in that department (Reference: Q2 — Department Salary Analysis, Q1 — Employee Directory)  
**Current value:** Example: R&D — 55,000 per employee, Sales — 62,500 per employee  
**Interpretation:** Measures how much the company invests in salaries per employee; high values may indicate skilled staff or overpaying.

## KPI 2: Project Staffing Ratio

**Definition:** Number of employees assigned to a project ÷ Total project roles (Reference: Q4 — Project Staffing Overview)  
**Current value:** Example: Project A — 5 employees, Project B — 3 employees, Project C — 0 employees  
**Interpretation:** Indicates whether projects have enough staff; a low ratio suggests potential understaffing.

## KPI 3: Employee Utilization Rate

**Definition:** Number of employees assigned to at least one project ÷ Total employees × 100% (Reference: Q7 — Unassigned Employees)  
**Current value:** Example: 42 out of 50 employees assigned → 84% utilization  
**Interpretation:** Shows how effectively employees are engaged; low utilization may reveal idle resources.

## KPI 4: Average Salary per Department vs Company Average

**Definition:** Average salary in a department ÷ Company-wide average salary (Reference: Q5 — Above-Average Departments)  
**Current value:** Example: R&D — 1.1× company average, HR — 0.95× company average  
**Interpretation:** Identifies departments with above-average pay; can guide budgeting and retention decisions.

## KPI 5: Monthly Hiring Trend

**Definition:** Number of employees hired per month (Reference: Q8 — Hiring Trends)  
**Current value:** Example: Jan 2024 — 3 hires, Feb 2024 — 2 hires, Mar 2024 — 2 hires  
**Interpretation:** Helps HR track recruitment flow and adjust hiring plans to meet staffing needs.
